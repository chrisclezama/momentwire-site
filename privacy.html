Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

Who we are

MomentWire is an independent sports intelligence and media project. This policy describes how we handle information for our public-source editorial research, including our Threads/Meta integration.

Information we may process

Where permitted by platform access and terms, we may process public posts and their text, public account identifiers or display names, timestamps, links, publicly available engagement information, and source references. We may also receive information you choose to provide when contacting us.

MomentWire does not intentionally access private Threads content through this use case. Public visibility does not mean unrestricted reuse or publication rights.

Why we use it

We handle data only for the stated editorial/intelligence purpose: topic and trend detection, recurring audience-question identification, public discussion recurrence, editorial research, and source provenance or audit. We distinguish audience demand from factual verification.

MomentWire does not sell personal data, use Threads data for targeted advertising, or use Threads data to infer sensitive personal characteristics.

Retention and protection

Public-source data is retained only as reasonably necessary for editorial analysis, provenance, audit, security, or legal obligations. We limit access to people and processing tools needed for these purposes and use safeguards appropriate to the information. No specific fixed retention period is represented by this policy.

Third-party services

We use hosting and processing services as needed for the stated purposes. Third-party platform data remains subject to the relevant platform’s terms and policies. This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical request information under its own privacy statement. We do not add analytics, advertising trackers, scripts, or cookies to this website.

Your requests

You may request access, correction, or deletion where applicable. Please provide enough information to identify the relevant data, such as a public profile or post URL. We may need to verify that you are entitled to make the request; do not send passwords or access tokens. We will assess requests subject to applicable obligations and data we control. See our data deletion instructions.
