Data Deletion

Effective September 27, 2026

To request deletion of data associated with MomentWire, contact us with the subject “Data Deletion Request.”

Contact

contact@momentwire.example

Placeholder address — not an active mailbox. A working contact address must be added before Meta app review submission.

What to include

  • A public profile or post URL, or another reference identifying the relevant data.
  • A brief description of your request and its connection to you.
  • A reply address so we can respond.

Do not send passwords, access tokens, government ID documents, or unnecessary sensitive information. If verification is needed, we will explain the minimum information required.

What happens next

We will assess your request, identify relevant records that we control, and delete data where applicable or explain any necessary retention or limitation. Limited records may need to be retained to comply with applicable obligations, security requirements, or to record fulfillment of the request.

MomentWire cannot delete your Threads, Meta, or other third-party account, remove data controlled by those platforms, or erase content from services we do not control. Use the relevant platform’s own account and privacy settings for those actions.

For more information, read our Privacy Policy.
